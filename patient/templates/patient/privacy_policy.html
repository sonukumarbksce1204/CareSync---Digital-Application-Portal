Privacy Policy

At CareSync, your medical privacy is our absolute priority. This policy outlines how we protect and manage your Personal Health ID and family records.

1. AI Verifications & Immutability

Once a medical record's AI diagnosis is reviewed by a licensed doctor, the record becomes a permanent medical artifact. It cannot be edited or deleted by the patient, ensuring data consistency for hospitals.

2. Doctor Access Logs

Doctors can only view your records if you grant them access via your Personal or Family Health ID. Every access request is securely logged in the system.

3. Data Archival

Records that have not been verified by a doctor can be archived ("soft-deleted") by the patient. Archival removes the record from your immediate view but preserves system integrity.
